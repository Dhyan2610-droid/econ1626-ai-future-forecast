# Australia's AI Economy 2030: Productivity Boom or Labour Market Shock?

## ECON1626 – Assessment 3: AI Future Forecast Interactive Web Essay

### Author

Dhyan Balasubramaniam

### Course

ECON1626 – Economics of Artificial Intelligence

### Overview

This project explores how artificial intelligence may reshape Australia's economy by 2030. Inspired by the AI 2027 forecasting framework, the interactive web essay analyses the economic mechanisms through which AI affects productivity, employment, industry structure and income distribution.

The project develops three future scenarios:

* Baseline Scenario – Managed but Uneven Transformation
* Upside Scenario – Productivity Renaissance
* Downside Scenario – Labour Market Shock

The report evaluates how different policy choices, governance frameworks and workforce adaptation strategies influence Australia's economic trajectory under increasing AI adoption.

---

## Research Question

Will artificial intelligence generate a productivity boom for Australia by 2030, or will labour market disruption and inequality outweigh the economic benefits?

## Methodology

This forecast uses scenario analysis rather than a single-point prediction due to the uncertainty surrounding AI development and adoption.

Evidence and assumptions were informed by:

* OECD AI Outlook
* OECD AI and Work
* International Monetary Fund (IMF)
* World Economic Forum (WEF)
* Jobs and Skills Australia
* Australian Treasury
* Australian Government National AI Plan
* McKinsey Global Institute
